Empowering learners through active engagement, technology, and innovative pedagogy.

What is Student-Centred Teaching?

Learner-Focused

Teaching that places the student at the centre of the learning experience, recognising their individual needs, interests, and prior knowledge.

Active Engagement

Students participate in discussions, problem-solving, and collaborative activities rather than passively receiving information.

Facilitator Role

The teacher shifts from being the sole source of knowledge to a guide who supports and encourages independent learning.

The Flipped Classroom Model

How It Works

The flipped classroom inverts traditional teaching by delivering instructional content outside class time and using face-to-face sessions for active learning.

  • At Home: Students watch video lectures, read materials, and complete preparatory quizzes.
  • In Class: Students engage in discussions, group projects, and hands-on problem solving.
  • Feedback Loop: Teachers provide immediate, personalised guidance during activities.

Key Benefits

01

Self-Paced Learning

Students control their own pace, revisiting content as needed.

02

Higher Engagement

Class time becomes interactive and meaningful.

03

Personalised Support

Teachers identify and address individual learning gaps.

Effective Teaching Strategies

★

Collaborative Learning

Students work in groups to solve problems, share ideas, and learn from each other through structured peer interaction.

◆

Formative Assessment

Ongoing quizzes, polls, and reflections help monitor progress and provide timely feedback to guide improvement.

◇

Problem-Based Learning

Real-world scenarios challenge students to apply theoretical knowledge to practical situations.

◆

Reflective Practice

Journals, exit tickets, and self-assessments encourage metacognition and ownership of learning.
